Launch & Auto-Updates

By default the client tracks the release channel on startup and while running. If an update goes live while you are in a match, a subtle CLIENT OUTDATED pill appears at the top of your screen instead of interrupting your game.

To test upcoming builds early, add -beta to your launch options (kept across automatic restarts). Open the latest changelog anytime in console with hnsru_changelog.
